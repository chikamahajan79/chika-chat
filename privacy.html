Privacy Policy for Chika

Application: Chika (com.chetan.chika)
Developer: Chetan (chetan@chetan.co)
Last Updated: November 2026
Summary: Chika is a zero-knowledge, end-to-end encrypted messaging application. We do not collect, track, store, or sell your personal messages, contact lists, or media files.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Phone Numbers or Real Names: Chika uses anonymous cryptographic keys and random identifiers exchanged directly between users (e.g., via QR code).
  • No Plaintext Messages or Media: All texts, voice notes, photos, and files are end-to-end encrypted on your device using the Signal Protocol (Double Ratchet + X3DH) and AES-256-GCM before transmission.
  • No Encryption Keys: Master keys and cryptographic ratchet states are stored exclusively in your device's secure hardware enclave (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). The developer has no access to your private keys.

2. Ephemeral Server Relay & Data Retention

  • In-Transit Ciphertext: When sending encrypted messages to an offline recipient, our relay server temporarily queues the unreadable ciphertext envelope.
  • Automated Instant Purge: As soon as the recipient device receives the message envelope and delivers an acknowledgment receipt (ACK), the envelope is permanently purged from server memory and databases.

3. Device Permissions & Biometric Security

  • Biometric Authentication (Fingerprint / Face Unlock): Processed 100% locally on your device by the operating system's secure biometric framework. Biometric data never leaves your device.
  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively on-device for scanning friend pairing QR codes and capturing photos you choose to send.
  • Microphone Permission: Used solely on-device for recording encrypted voice notes that you choose to send.

4. Third-Party Services & Notifications

Push notifications (via FCM / APNs) contain zero message body text and zero sender metadata to prevent third-party notification readers from intercepting your communication.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

Because Chika does not store user accounts, phone numbers, or unencrypted data on servers, deleting the app from your device permanently purges all local cryptographic keys and chat history.
